# MileLog privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026. Contact: **contact@collectoratlas.app**.

MileLog is a mileage recordkeeping app. It does not require an account or operate a server for your trip log. The developer does not receive your trip records through the app. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs.

**Information on your device.** The app stores trips, dates, purposes, locations, vehicle information, mileage, expenses, edit history, settings, feedback notes, and GPS drafts on your device. GPS recording retains distance and endpoints, not a complete route trace.

**Location.** Location is used for trips you explicitly start. Recording can continue while the screen is locked or another app is open, and stops when you pause or end the trip. The app does not automatically detect drives. You can revoke location access in iPhone Settings and continue using manual entry.

**Apple Maps.** Optional place lookup sends your search to Apple Maps. You can type locations without using lookup. Apple's services are covered by [Apple Maps & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/).

**Exports and backups.** You choose when and where to share CSV reports and backup files. A selected destination handles its copy under its own policies. Device backups may include app data according to your device settings. Protect backups because they contain locations and trip history.

**Retention and deletion.** Deleting a trip removes it from the active log while preserving its edit history in backups. Settings → Erase all local data removes the local log, history, vehicles, recordings, preferences, and local export/recovery files. Deleting the app also removes its local data. Copies exported to other apps, shared with other people, or stored in device backups must be managed separately.

**Support correspondence.** Information you choose to email to contact@collectoratlas.app is used to respond to your request. Avoid sending private destinations or full backups unless needed. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the same address.

**Changes.** This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version.
