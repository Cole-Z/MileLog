# MileLog support

Email **contact@collectoratlas.app** for help with MileLog. Include your app version (Settings), iPhone model, iOS version, what you did, and what you expected. Redact private destinations and send only information needed to explain the issue.

**Record a trip:** while parked, tap Start trip, allow While Using location access with Precise Location, and wait for an accurate fix. End while parked at your destination, check the distance, add a purpose, and save. Recording is intended to continue with the phone locked or navigation open. Signal loss, force quit, pauses, or system interruptions can leave gaps; review any recovered recording and correct its miles.

**Enter manually:** select Add manually and enter the route, date, miles, purpose, and vehicle. Round trip doubles the one-way distance once. Odometer difference uses total miles and turns off doubling.

**Export a report:** use Reports to select dates, category, vehicle, and rate basis. The CSV contains the same selected trips. Business is selected by default; personal trips are included only when chosen. Missing rates leave mileage amounts blank. Parking and tolls are separate.

**Protect your data:** back up regularly in Settings and save the file outside MileLog. Create an external backup before updating or deleting the app. Restore merges records and keeps newer edits. Backups from the original prototype may contain recordings that require a manual duplicate check.

**Privacy and deletion:** view Privacy policy in Settings. Erase all local data removes the local log and recovery copies after confirmation. Copies you saved elsewhere are separate.
