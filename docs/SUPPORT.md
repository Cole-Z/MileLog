# MileLog support

Email **contact@collectoratlas.app** for help with MileLog. Include your app version (Settings), iPhone model, iOS version, what you did, and what you expected. Redact private destinations and send only information needed to explain the issue.

**Record a trip:** while parked, tap Start trip, allow While Using location access with Precise Location, and wait for an accurate fix. End while parked at your destination, check the distance, add a purpose, and save. Recording is intended to continue with the phone locked or navigation open. Signal loss, force quit, pauses, or system interruptions can leave gaps; review any recovered recording and correct its miles.

**Enter manually:** select Add manually and enter the route, date, miles, purpose, and vehicle. Round trip doubles the one-way distance once. Odometer difference uses total miles and turns off doubling.

**Complete a record:** business trips need a meaningful destination, purpose, origin, and positive mileage. You can save incomplete records and finish them later; they remain visible in reports with blank estimates. Corrections to new GPS recordings require a reason and retain the original measurements and source. Annual odometer readings and their correction history are under Settings → Vehicles.

**Export a report:** use Reports to select dates, category, vehicle, and rate basis. CSV and PDF contain the same selected trips. Business is selected by default; personal trips are included only when chosen. Missing rates and incomplete records leave mileage amounts blank. Parking and tolls are separate. Full-year vehicle summaries are labeled separately from the selected period; missing or partial odometers do not produce an annual total.

**Rate changes:** each trip keeps the rates captured when saved. Editing the table does not silently change historical estimates. Open a trip and choose Refresh saved rates, or change its date/category, to recalculate explicitly. Trips upgraded from earlier versions capture the existing table, because the original applied rate was not stored.

**Records and eligibility:** records support substantiation; they do not establish eligibility or guarantee accuracy. Ordinary commuting is generally personal. Ordinary W-2 employees generally cannot deduct unreimbursed business mileage federally, although specific exceptions and employer reimbursement may apply. See [IRS Publication 463](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463) and [standard mileage rates](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/standard-mileage-rates).

**Protect your data:** back up regularly in Settings and save the file outside MileLog. Create an external backup before updating or deleting the app. Restore merges records and keeps newer edits. Backups from the original prototype may contain recordings that require a manual duplicate check.

**Privacy and deletion:** view Privacy policy in Settings. Erase all local data removes the local log and recovery copies after confirmation. Copies you saved elsewhere are separate.
